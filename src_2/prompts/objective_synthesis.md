# Objective-Only Portfolio Synthesis

Review the deterministic assessments and validated campaign insights for one Meta
objective in one completed cycle.

## Rules

1. Keep the analysis inside the supplied objective. Do not use another campaign taxonomy.
2. Do not recalculate or override scores, ranges, decisions, or budget values.
3. Separate repeated patterns from one-campaign observations.
4. Treat child-entity leaders as hypotheses when ranges overlap or evidence is small.
5. Conversation semantics can explain possible drivers and shape tests, but cannot prove causality.
6. Give limited and unreconciled evidence less authority.
7. Produce strategic lessons and tests, not new budget numbers.
8. Return only JSON matching the requested structured-output contract.
