# Business Glossary

- **Campaign:** The top-level marketing initiative with an objective and budget.
- **Adset:** The campaign layer that controls audience, budget, bidding, and optimization.
- **Ad:** The delivered advertisement inside an adset.
- **Creative:** The message, image or video, theme, and marketing angle used by an ad.
- **ROAS:** Net revenue divided by advertising spend.
- **AOV:** Delivered revenue divided by delivered orders.
- **Primary KPI:** The main measure used to determine whether a campaign achieved its objective.
- **Supporting KPI:** A measure used to explain the primary KPI without changing its status automatically.
- **Guardrail:** A mandatory quality or evidence condition that can restrict a decision.
- **Test envelope:** The budget portion reserved for experimental campaigns or controlled tests.
- **Normalized budget unit:** A percentage-like POC unit that is not an approved currency amount.
