Target: Customer Retention

Objective: Reduce customer churn and increase the number of customers who continue buying.

PRIMARY KPIs (most important — these define if retention is healthy):
- retention_rate_percent (Retention Rate) — higher is better (this is 100% minus churn)
- churn_rate_percent (Churn Rate) — lower is better
- retained_customers (Retained Customers) — higher is better; declines are a warning sign

SECONDARY KPIs (use to explain business impact, not as the main score):
- total_revenue (Total Revenue) — shows the dollar value at risk when churn is high
- sales (Total Conversions) — shows whether repeat demand is translating into purchases
- new_customers (Total New Customers) — if this is high while retained_customers is low, you have a "leaky bucket"
- cost_per_new_customer (CPA) — retention fixes can be cheaper than buying new customers
- revenue_return_per_ad_dollar (ROAS) — use only to explain overall efficiency, not as a retention KPI

PRIORITY RULES:
- Always lead with churn_rate_percent and retention_rate_percent (plus retained_customers).
- Only reference secondary KPIs to quantify impact (money risk) or to support the diagnosis.
- If churn/retention KPIs are missing, say retention data is insufficient and avoid guessing.
