Target: Revenue Growth

Objective: Increase revenue while maintaining healthy return on ad spend.

PRIMARY KPIs (most important — these define if revenue is healthy):
- revenue_return_per_ad_dollar (ROAS) — benchmark: 4x+ good, 2–4x warning, <2x bad
- marketing_roi_percent (Marketing ROI) — benchmark: >100% good, negative = losing money
- cost_per_new_customer (CPA) — lower is better relative to annual_value_per_customer

SECONDARY KPIs (use to explain root cause of Primary KPI problems):
- revenue_per_click → low = low AOV or low conversion rate
- average_order_value (AOV) → low = customers not spending enough per order
- annual_value_per_customer → compare against CPA to judge long-term profitability
- annual_value_to_cost_ratio (LTV:CAC) → below 1x = losing money per customer
- purchase_rate_percent (Conversion Rate) → low = traffic not converting

PRIORITY RULES:
- Always lead with Primary KPIs.
- Only reference Secondary KPIs to explain why a Primary KPI is good or bad.
- Never flag a Secondary KPI as a problem if all Primary KPIs are healthy.
- If Marketing ROI is negative, state exact dollar loss per $1 spent.
- If LTV:CAC is below 1x, state exact gap between customer value and acquisition cost.
